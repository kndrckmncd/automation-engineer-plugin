# Manulife Performance Rating Guide

## Rating Scale

### The "What" (Objectives)

| Rating | Definition |
|--------|-----------|
| **Exceptional** | Surpassing your objectives with excellent results and impact. Consistently exceeds expectations of the role and influences others to perform better. |
| **Highly Effective** | Achieving your objectives with strong results and impact. Performing beyond the core expectations of the role. |
| **Effective** | Performing well against most or all objectives with good results and impact. Achieving the core expectations for the role. |
| **Developing** | Performing inconsistently against objectives with room to improve results and impact. Additional direction and support needed. |

### The "How" (Values & Risk)

| Rating | Values | Influence | Risk |
|--------|--------|-----------|------|
| **Exceptional** | Champion and empower others to demonstrate Values | Beyond department/business | Champion risk awareness in carrying out work |
| **Highly Effective** | Strongly demonstrate Values | Beyond your team | Strongly demonstrate risk awareness |
| **Effective** | Demonstrate Values | Within your team | Demonstrate risk awareness |
| **Developing** | Room to improve how you demonstrate Values | Room to improve | Room to improve risk awareness |

> **Important**: The lower of the two ratings (What vs How) determines compensation decisions.

## SMART Goal Format for Performance Reviews

| Criterion | Guidance |
|-----------|---------|
| **Specific** | Name the project, system, or deliverable clearly |
| **Measurable** | Include ticket count, deployment count, scripts built, % improvement |
| **Attainable** | Reflect scope realistic for role level and team size |
| **Results-oriented** | State the business outcome: efficiency, reliability, speed, cost saving |
| **Time-bound** | Reference specific quarters (Q1–Q4) or dates |

## Risk Management Questions (for "How" section)

1. Does the colleague demonstrate awareness and understanding of the risks associated with their work?
2. Does the colleague proactively and quickly let their manager know when something goes wrong?
3. Does the colleague take the initiative to raise issues with their manager and discuss whether risks are within appetite?
4. Has the colleague contributed to improving risk management practices related to their role?
